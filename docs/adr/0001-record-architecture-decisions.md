# 1. Record architecture decisions

Date: 2026-11-08

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Decisions get lost when people move on. DfE and GDS teams record significant technical decisions
as Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) next to the code.

## Decision

We will keep short ADRs in `docs/adr`, numbered, one decision each, with context and consequences.

## Consequences

New team members can see why things are the way they are. A changed decision gets a new ADR that
supersedes the old one, so history is kept.
